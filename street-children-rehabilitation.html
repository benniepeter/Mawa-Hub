REHABILITATION PATHWAY

From immediate safety to long-term opportunity.

1. Safe referral

Receive concerns and route them to an authorised safeguarding partner or competent authority.

2. Protection assessment

Qualified professionals assess immediate safety, family circumstances and support needs.

3. Safe accommodation

Where appropriate, connect children to vetted, lawful safe-care arrangements through authorised partners.

4. Education

Support school access, catch-up learning and practical skills pathways appropriate to age and circumstances.

5. Family/community support

Where safe and appropriate, work with families and community services toward sustainable reintegration.

6. Follow-up

Monitor outcomes through authorised case workers and approved partner organisations.
