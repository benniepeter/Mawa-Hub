From streets to safety. From survival to opportunity.

Street-connected children deserve protection, dignity, education and a safe pathway forward. MawaHub's framework brings reporting, referral, rehabilitation, family support and long-term reintegration into one child-centred pathway.

THE ISSUE

A child on the street is a child who needs a pathway to safety.

Children living or working on streets can face poverty, family separation, interrupted education, violence, exploitation, unsafe housing, substance exposure and stigma. The response must protect the child without exposing them to more harm.

🇰🇪 Kenya

Child-centred outreach, safeguarding assessment, family tracing, education catch-up, school reintegration, psychosocial support, health referrals and age-appropriate skills pathways.

Kenya Reporting Centre

🇹🇿 Tanzania

Community referrals, safeguarding, family and community assessment, school access, alternative education, health and psychosocial referrals and safe pathways for older youth.
