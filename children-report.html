What happens next?

1. Receive

The report is received through the selected channel.

2. Triage

Urgency and safeguarding risks should be assessed by trained personnel.

3. Refer

Where appropriate, connect the child to government child protection, police, health, shelter, legal or psychosocial services.

4. Follow up

Case follow-up should use secure records, consent/assent where appropriate, and the best interests of the child.
