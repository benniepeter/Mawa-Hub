REPORTING MODEL

How accountability works

1. Receive

Record verified donations, grants, marketplace impact contributions and partner funding.

2. Allocate

Assign resources according to approved programs, budgets and restrictions.

3. Deliver

Track project activities, beneficiaries, procurement and operational delivery.

4. Verify

Maintain supporting records and independent review where applicable.

5. Report

Publish financial and impact summaries in clear, accessible language.

6. Improve

Use evidence, community feedback and audits to strengthen future programs.
