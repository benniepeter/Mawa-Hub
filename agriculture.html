FARMING KNOWLEDGE

Practical dondoo for farmers.

🐟 Fish farming

Plan ponds, feeding, water quality, stocking, fish health, harvesting and market access. VICKTECHFISH materials describe tilapia, catfish, Nile perch, silver cyprinid, Clarias and lungfish as key fish categories in its Lake Victoria value-chain model.

💧 Water quality

Monitor water conditions and avoid overfeeding. Advanced systems described in the VICKTECHFISH material include filtration, biological treatment, UV disinfection and remote monitoring.

⚙️ Smart ponds

Aerators, automatic feeders and water-level controls can improve consistency and reduce labour when correctly designed and maintained.

☀️ Solar + aquaculture

Explore solar-powered farm operations and integrated energy solutions that can reduce operating costs where technically and financially suitable.

🌱 Climate-smart agriculture

Learn soil, water, crop, waste and energy practices that improve resilience while protecting natural resources.

♻️ Zero-waste value chains

Fish by-products such as scales, skin, oil and bones can have secondary value when safely processed for appropriate markets.
